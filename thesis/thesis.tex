\chapter*{Abstract}
Structure-aware fuzzers like Nautilus use their own grammar formats. These formats are often poorly supported, because they are useless outside of one specific fuzzer. Other formats are much better supported. One example is ANTLR. It has a large community and many well-maintained grammars in repositories like grammars-v4. It would be useful to use these grammars in tools with less common formats like Nautilus. This thesis presents Babel, a unified grammar translator for context-free fuzzing grammars. Babel translates ANTLR grammars to Nautilus in bulk. This way, Nautilus can benefit from the existing ANTLR grammars. Babel is also modular. Users can add their own adapters to translate to and from other grammar formats.
To evaluate the feasibility and performance of Babel i tested the generated Nautilus Grammars against 957 grammars from the grammars-v4 repository to verify accuracy, translatability and perfornace against a ANTLR native fuzzer
